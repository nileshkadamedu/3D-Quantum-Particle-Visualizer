Theory

Energy Levels

For a particle confined in a 3D box of dimensions \(L_x \times L_y \times L_z\), the allowed energy is:

\[ E_{n_x,n_y,n_z}=\frac{\pi^2\hbar^2}{2m}\left(\frac{n_x^2}{L_x^2}+\frac{n_y^2}{L_y^2}+\frac{n_z^2}{L_z^2}\right) \]

Normalized Wavefunction

The stationary-state wavefunction inside the box is:

\[ \psi_{n_x,n_y,n_z}(x,y,z)=\sqrt{\frac{8}{L_xL_yL_z}}\,\sin\left(\frac{n_x\pi x}{L_x}\right)\sin\left(\frac{n_y\pi y}{L_y}\right)\sin\left(\frac{n_z\pi z}{L_z}\right) \]

Time-Dependent Wavefunction

The time evolution of a stationary energy state is represented by:

\[ \psi(x,y,z,t)=\psi(x,y,z)e^{-iEt/\hbar} \]
